## Reflection on the Use of Artificial Intelligence

Throughout this semester, AI has helped me understand object-oriented programming concepts and improve my approach to software development. One benefit of using AI is that it can explain concepts in simpler terms and help me understand how different parts of a program work together. For example, while working on the CourseRegistration midterm project, I used AI to understand how to separate responsibilities into different classes, such as the registration service, tuition calculator, and repository. This helped me understand the importance of separation of concerns and how organizing code into classes makes a program easier to maintain and update.

However, I have also learned that AI is not always accurate or completely aligned with assignment requirements. For example, AI can suggest classes, methods, or design decisions that make sense in general but may not match the specific code or instructions provided by the professor. This taught me that I cannot automatically assume that AI-generated responses are correct. I need to compare its suggestions with the assignment instructions, review the code, and make sure I understand why a particular solution works instead of simply copying the answer.

Moving forward, I plan to continue using AI as a learning tool in future courses and professional software development. I want to use it to explain unfamiliar concepts, help me identify errors, review my code, and explore different approaches to solving problems. At the same time, I want to strengthen my own programming skills by writing code independently, practicing problem-solving, and testing my solutions before asking AI for help. My goal is to use AI to support my learning rather than depend on it to complete my work. This will help me become a more confident programmer who can understand, develop, and maintain software independently.
